# KPI Suite research basis

The KPI catalogue combines the organisation’s supplied monthly tracker with a wider set of management measures. The additional measures are grouped around service delivery, safety, workforce assurance, people’s experience and outcomes, and governance.

The design was informed by current official sources:

- CQC assessment framework: monitoring and improving outcomes
  https://www.cqc.org.uk/guidance-regulation/providers/assessment/single-assessment-framework/effective/monitoring-improving-outcomes
- CQC assessment framework: governance, management and sustainability
  https://www.cqc.org.uk/guidance-regulation/providers/assessment/single-assessment-framework/well-led/governance-management-sustainability
- CQC assessment framework: safe systems, pathways and transitions
  https://www.cqc.org.uk/guidance-regulation/providers/assessment/single-assessment-framework/safe/safe-systems-pathways-transitions
- CQC assessment framework: listening to and involving people
  https://www.cqc.org.uk/guidance-regulation/providers/assessment/single-assessment-framework/responsive/listening-involving-people
- CQC Regulation 12 guidance: safe care and treatment
  https://www.cqc.org.uk/guidance-regulation/providers/regulations-service-providers-and-managers/health-social-care-act/regulation-12
- Department of Health and Social Care: Adult Social Care Outcomes Framework handbook
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/adult-social-care-outcomes-framework-handbook-of-definitions/the-adult-social-care-outcomes-framework-handbook-of-definitions
- Skills for Care: recruitment and retention measures
  https://www.skillsforcare.org.uk/Adult-Social-Care-Workforce-Data/workforceintelligence/Monthly-statistics/Recruitment-and-retention.aspx
- Skills for Care: workforce-data glossary
  https://www.skillsforcare.org.uk/Adult-Social-Care-Workforce-Data/workforceintelligence/About-our-data/Glossary.aspx
- NHS England: Patient Safety Incident Response Framework
  https://www.england.nhs.uk/patient-safety/patient-safety-insight/incident-response-framework/

These sources support the domains and measure selection; they do not prescribe one universal threshold for every provider. Default targets in QCGMS are starting points and should be reviewed against the organisation’s service model, risk appetite, contracts and internal definitions.
