# Controller, processor and supplier assurance

The subscribing care provider is expected to act as controller for information it
places in QCGMS. The contracted QCGMS service operates as processor only on the
controller's documented instructions, except where law establishes a separate
purpose. Roles must be confirmed contractually for each deployment.

Before live use, the agreement must record subject matter and duration; nature and
purpose; data and people; controller instructions; confidentiality; security;
subprocessors/change notice; transfers; rights and incident assistance; DPIA and
regulator assistance; deletion/return; audit rights; availability and exit.

Do not infer a supplier from source code or marketing. The production owner must
maintain an approved register containing legal entity, service, data, region,
transfer mechanism, security evidence, DPA, incident route, continuity, exit and
review date. Hosting, database, storage, email, monitoring, support and any future
AI/integration provider must be recorded before handling customer data.
