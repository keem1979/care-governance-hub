# Product and Customer Success Measures

QCGMS must prove operational value rather than count pages or forms. Measures
use real tenant-scoped events and never fabricate missing values.

## Customer outcome measures

| Measure | Definition | Initial pilot target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High-risk action ownership | Open high/critical actions with owner and due date / all open high/critical actions | At least 95% |
| Verified on-time closure | High/critical actions independently verified by due date / high/critical actions due in period | At least 90% |
| Evidence-backed closure | Closed actions with required evidence and verification / all closed actions | 100% where evidence is required |
| Effectiveness completion | Actions reaching effectiveness review / actions due for effectiveness review | At least 90% |
| Recurrence rate | Repeat findings in monitoring period / verified closures monitored | Baseline then reduce |
| Care change acknowledgement | Required workers completing the current material-change control / workers required | At least 80% in pilot; safety deadlines may require higher local targets |
| Evidence currency | Verified evidence within its validity rule / evidence required for current framework scope | At least 90% |
| External dependency ageing | Median and maximum days awaiting an external response, with current interim control | Visible for every open external dependency |
| Inspection pack preparation | Median manager time to produce a traceable pack | Reduce by at least 50% from baseline |
| Evidence retrieval | Median time to reach source evidence from a management indicator | Under three interactions |

## Product adoption measures

- Weekly active Registered Managers among invited RMs.
- Percentage of priority workflows completed without support.
- Form abandonment by step, excluding sensitive content from analytics.
- Validation-error frequency by field.
- Median time from finding creation to ownership, evidence and verification.
- Duplicate identity candidates and time to reconciliation.
- Repeated manual entry reported or detected between modules.
- Notification open, dismissal and escalation rates.
- Implementation checklist completion before customer go-live.

## Safety and trust measures

- Cross-tenant access test failures: target zero.
- Critical and high security findings open at release: target zero.
- High-severity clinical hazards open at release: target zero.
- Backup restoration exercises completed successfully.
- AI answers with displayed authorised citations.
- AI uncertainty correctly escalated in the controlled test set.
- Configuration changes with complete approval history.

## Measurement rules

- Show `No data recorded` when a valid denominator does not exist.
- Do not combine incomparable locations or service types without explanation.
- Preserve the source records and calculation version behind every displayed measure.
- Never convert these measures into a predicted regulator rating.
- Product analytics must record workflow metadata, not confidential narrative content.
