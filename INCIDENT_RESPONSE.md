# Security and personal-data incident response

## Immediate actions

1. Protect people and continuity of care; switch to approved downtime processes
   where system availability or data integrity could affect safe work.
2. Report internally at once. Preserve logs, timestamps, affected identifiers and
   evidence; do not edit or delete suspected material.
3. Contain proportionately: revoke sessions, disable affected accounts, rotate
   secrets, isolate an integration or pause a release.
4. Appoint incident lead, technical lead, data-protection lead, clinical-safety
   lead where relevant, customer liaison and decision recorder.

Record what happened, discovery time, affected tenants/people, data categories,
confidentiality/integrity/availability effect, safety impact, attack path, ongoing
risk, containment, notification decisions, restoration evidence and owners.
The controller and processor assess contractual and legal notification duties
promptly using the facts; QCGMS does not automatically decide notification duties.

Restore from a known-good point, reconcile records changed during downtime,
validate tenant isolation and access, obtain business and clinical approval, then
monitor closely. Link root cause and corrective actions to evidence, independent
verification, effectiveness review and recurrence monitoring.
